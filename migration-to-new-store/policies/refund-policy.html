Return & Refund Policy

We want you to enjoy your gummies. If something isn't right, here's how we fix it.

Once the seal is broken, the pack cannot be returned

GummyChums products are consumable nutraceutical products. If the seal on a pack has been broken, that pack cannot be returned, exchanged or refunded under any circumstances. This applies whether the pack is full, partly eaten or untouched after opening.

This is not a preference. Once a food or supplement pack has been opened we have no way to verify how it has been stored or handled, and it can never be resold or redistributed. The rule is standard for food and nutraceutical products in India and protects every customer who buys from us, including you.

Please check your order before you open anything. A wrong item, a damaged pouch or a short-dated pack is all visible with the seal intact. Film your unboxing, and if something looks wrong, stop and contact us before opening.

When you can return a product

We accept returns and issue refunds or replacements in the following cases:

  • Damaged in transit — the pouch or outer packaging arrived crushed, torn, or leaking.
  • Wrong item received — you got a different product or flavour than the one you ordered.
  • Missing items — part of your order didn't arrive.
  • Expired or near-expiry stock — you received a pack that has expired, or expires unusually soon.
  • Manufacturing defect — a genuine quality issue with the product itself.

You have 48 hours

Raise your request within 48 hours of delivery. Nothing can be processed after that.

The window is short for a reason. Every case we accept is visible the moment the parcel arrives, with the seal still intact — there is nothing to discover later. Couriers also close their own claim window within days, so after 48 hours we can no longer raise it on your behalf either.

We need an uncut unboxing video

Start filming before anything is opened, and keep recording in one continuous take until the pack is out and visible. We cannot accept a return without it.

The video must:

  • be uncut and unedited — one continuous recording, not clips joined together;
  • begin before the outer packaging is opened, showing the sealed parcel and the shipping label;
  • be clear enough to read the shipping label, and the batch and expiry printed on the pack.

Our team reviews every video that comes in. Whether it establishes the claim is at GummyChums' discretion, and that decision is final.

We know filming an unboxing is a small nuisance. It is also the only way to tell a pack that arrived damaged from one damaged after it arrived, and it is what the courier requires from us before they will pay a claim. Half a minute of video is what makes a genuine claim payable.

How to raise a return

  1. Email support@gummychums.com.
  2. Attach the uncut unboxing video, your order number, and what went wrong.
  3. Add clear photos of the product and packaging too, including the batch and expiry printed on the pack.
  4. We'll confirm within 2 working days and arrange a reverse pickup where applicable.

Please keep the product and all its packaging until your request is resolved. Returns sent back without prior confirmation cannot be accepted.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect the returned item — or approve your claim on the basis of the photos — we'll process your refund.

  • Prepaid orders (UPI, cards, net banking, wallets): refunded to the original payment method. We initiate the refund within 24 hours of approval; your bank or provider typically credits it in 5–7 working days.
  • Cash on Delivery orders: refunded via bank transfer through our logistics partner's returns process. We aim to complete COD refund payouts within 72 hours of approval, subject to the details you provide being correct.

Original shipping charges are non-refundable, except where the return is our fault (damaged, wrong, or defective item) — in which case we refund the full amount including shipping.

Replacements

If you'd prefer a replacement over a refund, just tell us. We'll ship a fresh pack at no cost to you, subject to stock availability.

Cancellations

You can cancel an order any time before it's dispatched — contact us as soon as possible and we'll cancel it and refund you in full. Once an order has been dispatched, it can no longer be cancelled; you'll need to follow the return process above instead.

If your order comes back to us undelivered

Sometimes a parcel returns to us without ever being delivered — nobody was available across the courier's attempts, the address was incomplete, the phone number didn't connect, or the delivery was refused at the door.

When that happens we refund what you paid for the gummies. The delivery charge is not refunded, because the courier has already made the journey and we have already paid for it.

If the parcel came back because of something we got wrong — we shipped to the wrong address, or the courier lost or damaged it on the way — you get everything back, delivery included. That one is on us.

If you chose cash on delivery, nothing was charged, so there is nothing to refund.

The easiest way to avoid all of this: check your address and phone number before you place the order, and keep your phone reachable on the delivery day. Couriers call before they arrive.

What we can't refund

  • Any pack whose seal has been broken — opened, used or partially consumed. There are no exceptions to this.
  • Products returned without prior confirmation from us.
  • Requests raised after the 48-hour window.
  • Claims submitted without an uncut unboxing video, or with a video our team cannot verify.
  • Products damaged by misuse or improper storage after delivery.
  • A change of mind about taste or flavour preference, where the pack has been opened.
